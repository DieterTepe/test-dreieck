Common tasks

Find the area, angles and height of a triangle

Three questions come up again and again in the workshop, in design and at school. Here are the matching formulas – DT-ProfiDreieck picks the calculation path automatically and shows it step by step.

Area of a triangle – even without the height

With base and height it is simple. If only the three sides are known, Heron's formula does the job; with two sides and the included angle, the sine. This also works for an irregular triangle without a right angle.

A = ½ · c · hc
A = ½ · a · b · sin γ
A = √(s·(s−a)·(s−b)·(s−c)), s = (a+b+c)/2

Angles of a triangle from three sides

If all side lengths are given, the law of cosines returns every angle. If only one angle is missing, the angle sum of 180° is enough. With two sides and a non-included angle (SSA), the program checks whether there are zero, one or two solutions.

cos α = (b² + c² − a²) / (2·b·c)
α + β + γ = 180°
a / sin α = b / sin β = c / sin γ

Height of a triangle – from sides or an angle

The height (altitude) is perpendicular to a side. If an angle is known, the sine is enough. If only the three sides are given, the area is calculated first and the height follows from it. DT-ProfiDreieck shows all three heights and draws them to scale.

hc = b · sin α
hc = 2 · A / c

How do I calculate a triangle?

Choose three known values – for example three sides, two sides and an angle, or a combination with area, height, inradius or circumradius. DT-ProfiDreieck calculates the remaining values as long as the input defines a solution.

Which values does the triangle calculator provide?

Depending on the input: side lengths, interior angles, perimeter, area, heights, inradius and circumradius.

Can I export the triangle as DXF?

Yes, in the full version. The DXF export uses real units and separate layers for triangle, circles, construction lines and labels – ready for CAD and CNC.
